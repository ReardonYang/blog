### 🧰 A collection of commonly used AI tools:

* [MyGPT](https://www.3570000.xyz/) : A personal AI assistant based on GPT-3.5-turbo, fast

* [ChatGPT Shortcut](https://www.aishort.top/) : A comprehensive collection of prompts

* [Summarize](https://www.summarize.tech/) : Generates summaries of YouTube videos

* [MiniGPT-4](https://minigpt-4.github.io/) : Integrates GPT-4 capabilities to recognize semantics in images and videos

* [Auto-GPT](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/Auto-GPT) : Based on OpenAI's GPT-4 or GPT-3.5 API, it breaks any given goal down into subtasks and automatically loops through the Internet and other tools to achieve it

* [DeepL Translator](https://www.deepl.com/) : Provides translation between various languages, with direct translation of ppt, docx and pdf files

* [AI Toolset](https://ai-bot.cn/) : A collection of all kinds of AI tools and applications
