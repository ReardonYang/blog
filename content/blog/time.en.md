The age surges and rushes on, and I am blurred of face.

I open Xiami Music, and by chance it is Lo Ta-yu — "The Story of Time."

> The flowers of spring blooming, the wind of autumn
> 
> And the setting sun of winter
> 
> Melancholy, the youthful me
> 
> Once so innocently thought this way
> 
> The windmill, in the song of the four seasons turning,
> 
> Revolves day after day
> 
> In verses of wind, flower, snow and moon
> 
> I grow year after year
> 
> The flowing water carries away the story of time
> 
> And changes a person
> 
> Right there in that sentimental, first
> 
> Waiting youth
> 
> ......

Zhu Zhisan, the Nine Eccentrics of Yangzhou, the Twenty-Seven Hairpins of Jinling — rings of smoke rising, mist spreading. Still I do not know how to place myself, or how to treat well those beside me.
