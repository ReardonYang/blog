* When might Bitcoin accidentally hurt you? Only when you hold enough fiat and enough shitcoins!

* Every time Bitcoin and the cryptocurrencies fall, it is because those who have finished fleecing the newcomers are discarding their tools, cashing out and leaving the scene; at such moments the price naturally takes a discount. The sickle is still the same sickle — the question is whether you'll pick it up, and whether you have the stomach to harvest other people's leeks with it. Everyone must keep a clear head when looking at things.

* Blockchain's most successful application is Bitcoin, and Bitcoin's most mature use case is fleecing the newcomers.

* As long as Bitcoin can go on being used in the fleecing business, Bitcoin will not die.

* Old comrade Satoshi never imagined that this thing he casually tinkered into existence — Bitcoin — would blight tens of millions and bless tens of millions. Some cry for its blood; others savour it with delight.

* Bitcoin is the finest, sharpest sickle Satoshi gave us retail investors. This sickle cuts through iron like mud; what it severs is the lifeline of the money-printing machine, and what it wins for us is the sanctity and inviolability of private property.

* Dogecoin produced its first block in December 2013; Ethereum produced its first block in July 2015. So Dogecoin is actually senior to Ethereum.

* Under judicial pressure, PayPal will, under various powerful wills, close the accounts of specific users on its platform. But Bitcoin doesn't play that game. Bitcoin is meta-violence, and meta-violence has no leanings. Both righteous and wicked.

* We defend only what we deeply love; we deeply love only what we understand; we understand only what we are familiar with; we are familiar only with what we are trapped in. The coins that can trap a broad mass of people are the ones with the brightest future.
