**Administrator from the Imperial Center** — Lord Siyanien, as long as we all keep believing, as long as we keep using your long-abandoned **imperial legal currency — the Earthollar**, you will find in us those traits you despise; you think us **stupid, stubborn, easily incited**. But we **hate bitcoin**, just as you hate the Earthollar; we hate smart contracts, we hate everything digital; we are close to the soil, the trees, the rivers. On **the edge of the Empire, in the desert of the universe**, we have existed for tens of thousands of years. Among all the planets under imperial rule, we are perhaps **the most insignificant of all**, and we need to preserve this little uniqueness we have.

**————We have the freedom not to use bitcoin!**
