Walking is my goal

The goal I believe in

Walking on the road — rocks, weeds, branches

Being with myself, no friends, no entertainment, no work

This is all of time, my own time

No time left to worry.

--

Do not rush about toiling, do not speak in clichés

Enjoy the feeling of walking

The feeling of being with myself

Not full of suspicion, not hurrying along

This is all of time, my own time

No time left to worry

--

I ask for nothing else

The desires that make me dizzy and vexed

No books, no films, no music

On the road, watch the starry sky, listen to the sound of water

This is all of time, my own time

No time left to worry

--

I stand with the selves of yesterday and today, to greet the self of tomorrow

Feel with eyes, nose, mouth, ears, fingers

I feel all of this; they are all in my heart; I am filled with wild joy

This is all of time, my own time

No time left to worry
