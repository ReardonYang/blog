**On November 22, 9120 AD**, at the **Cultural Relics Auction of the Guangdong-Hong Kong Prefecture, West Pacific Communist United Community**, a **100-yuan note** printed on November 22, 2019 fetched the staggering price of one bitcoin, leaving everyone present in amazement.

It was only then that people understood: **the renminbi is a work of art, a symbol of human wisdom, while bitcoin is merely the most ordinary of digital assets**!
