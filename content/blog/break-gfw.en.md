### 1. Lantern

The reason Lantern comes first is simple: when you are completely cut off from the outside internet, or on a brand-new computer with no circumvention tools at all, you can download it from GitHub with no complicated configuration — install it and it works. Lantern provides free traffic, 1 GB per month; you can also buy the paid version, though I personally don't recommend it, since there are cheaper, more stable options.

In many situations, when I'm using someone else's computer and need to reach the outside internet right away, I tend to open GitHub, search for Lantern, and use it straight after installing.

Note: The iOS version requires a non-mainland-China (US/Hong Kong) account.

Supported operating systems: iOS, MacOS, Windows, Android.

### 2. ClashX & Shadowrocket & V2Ray

**The ones listed here are what I actually use day to day:**

* On iOS, use: [Shadowrocket](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/shadowrocket/id932747118)

* On MacOS, use: [ClashX](https://clashx.org/)

* On Windows, use: [V2Ray](https://www.v2ray.com/)

* On Android, use: [V2RayNG](https://v2rayng.org/)

**All of the above need to be paired with nodes, so strictly speaking they are really the same method. Here's how to get nodes:**

Paid nodes: [One-Yuan Airport](https://xn--4gq62f52gdss.com/#/register?code=30h3eFWK)

Free nodes: [Click to get them](https://v2rayshare.com/p/1454.html)
