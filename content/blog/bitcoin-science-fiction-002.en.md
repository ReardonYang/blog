**On December 11, 3140 AD**, a power outage occurred at the Bitcoin **Hash Power Bureau** of the **East Pacific Smart Contract Federation**. That day happened to be exactly 1,000 years after the last bitcoin was mined. The blackout lasted a full 7 hours, costing miners worldwide 24 BTC in lost bookkeeping revenue — 3% of the annual GDP of the West Pacific Feudal United Community.

Investigations found that the outage was caused by a hacker attack on the smart contract of the **Entropy China Interstellar Power Dispatch System**. The **Cyber Defense Corps** is still conducting further investigations.
