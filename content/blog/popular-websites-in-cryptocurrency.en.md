### ✔️ [BlockChair](https://blockchair.com/)

* About: A blockchain search and analytics engine for Bitcoin, Dogecoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Cardano, Stellar, EOS, Monero, Tezos, Dash, Zcash and other digital currencies. Filter and sort blocks, transactions and their contents to your own needs, and run full-text searches over blockchain content.
* Website: [blockchair.com](https://blockchair.com/)

### ✔️ [Infinite Market Cap](https://8marketcap.com/)

* About: Ranks the world's top assets by market cap, including cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, precious metals such as gold and silver, and listed companies such as Apple and Tesla.
* Website: [8marketcap.com](https://8marketcap.com/)

-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-

### ✔️ [FiatMarketCap](https://fiatmarketcap.com/)

* About: Ranks the fiat currencies of countries around the world by market cap — and includes Bitcoin besides. Imagine if Bitcoin were the de facto global reserve of value. Then how many satoshis (Sats) would one unit of each country's fiat buy? You can find the interesting answer here!
* Website: [fiatmarketcap.com](https://fiatmarketcap.com/)

-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-₿-

### ✔️ [mempool](https://mempool.space/)

* About: A blockchain explorer built for the Bitcoin community, offering commonly used Bitcoin API tools, focused on the Bitcoin fee market and Bitcoin's multi-layer protocol ecosystem, with no ads, no shitcoins and no third-party trackers.
* Website: [mempool.space](https://mempool.space/)

### ✔️ [Bitcoin Wiki](https://bitcoin.it/)

* About: An encyclopaedia of Bitcoin for Bitcoin users, developers, related businesses and anyone interested in Bitcoin. As the site's material is freely editable by the public, readers should note that what it provides does not represent the views of the entire Bitcoin community, and may at times be outdated, incorrect, or malicious.
* Website: [bitcoin.it](https://bitcoin.it/)
