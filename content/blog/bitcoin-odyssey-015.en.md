* As a trader, if you pay too much attention to the macro picture, it may cloud your judgment. And if you are a hodler who pays too much attention to short-term swings, your judgment will suffer just the same.

* A hodler does not wait for the best position; a hodler keeps buying. A hodler has only one move — "buy" — so a hodler's advice does not suit a trader.

* Bitcoin has no circuit breaker. Any price is possible.

* If you are bullish on Bitcoin, an empty position is a disgrace: an empty position means you have lost contact with Bitcoin.

* An extreme asset must be met with matching, extreme methods. There is no room for gentle breezes and soft rain. Revolution is not a dinner party.

* Steamed buns and pickles to improve life; bitcoin to save.

* Dare to be trapped in a position. It is a virtue.

* If you avoid the risk, you avoid the wealth as well.

* You need to hold for at least four years before you can feel the charm of the Bitcoin market.

* Many people think Bitcoin's rise is unreasonable. You think it unreasonable because you have not seen the reason. You may think a woman's ample breasts have grown unreasonable — but if you had truly held them in your hands, stroked them, felt their warmth, you would find them perfectly reasonable, perfectly real.

* Pushing the price up is for pushing it higher still. Just like eating: eating is for getting full.
