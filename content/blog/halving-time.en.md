Every 210,000 blocks Bitcoin produces (roughly four years), the block reward is cut in half. Starting from 50 BTC at the genesis block in 2009, after 33 halvings the reward reaches zero around 2140, and the total supply of bitcoin ultimately converges on 21 million coins.
